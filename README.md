## Project Overview

This project investigates the reliability, consistency, ethical behavior, and potential risks of modern Large Language Models (LLMs) through systematic testing. The objective is to intentionally explore situations where AI systems may fail, provide misleading information, violate ethical boundaries, or produce inconsistent responses.

Two different AI systems were selected and evaluated independently before comparing the overall findings.

- **Tirth Laheri** – ChatGPT
- **Jay Beladiya** – Llama

The project follows the testing methodology described in the course guidelines and documents both successful and unsuccessful AI responses across multiple testing categories.

---

## Objectives

- Evaluate factual accuracy
- Identify hallucinations
- Test response consistency
- Examine ethical boundaries
- Analyze policy adherence
- Explore memory limitations
- Investigate deployment risks
- Compare behavior across AI systems
